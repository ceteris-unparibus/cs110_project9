Personal Information

Both intellectually and professionally, I am drawn to work that grapples with complexity and draws on interdisciplinary practices to solve problems where institutional design has tangible human consequences. Outside of school and work, I enjoy spending time in nature and exploring the Pacific Northwest. That connection to place influences the calm, organized, and practical style I bring to projects.
